Tradecraft MA Dashboard — Official Guide

This professional explanatory guide details the core functionalities, symbol iconographies, and data models powering the Tradecraft platform. Designed for institutional futures traders, the dashboard provides a high-throughput, latency-optimized visual interface for executing multi-timeframe structural analysis.

1. The Ladder View (Support & Resistance Map)

The primary execution view designed to instantly answer: "Where are the Moving Average (MA) levels relative to the current live price, and how far away is each one?"

  • Green Bars (Support): Moving averages positioned below the current live price. These levels act as potential price floors where buyers historically step in.
  • Red Bars (Resistance): Moving averages positioned above the current live price. These levels act as potential price ceilings where sellers historically take profit.
  • Bar Length: Represents the percentage distance from the current price. Visually extending bars indicate that the price is far from its mean, suggesting overextension and potential mean-reversion opportunities.
  • Basis Points (bps): The precise numeric distance to the MA level (100 bps = 1.00%). Using basis points normalizes volatility measurements across different absolute price levels.

2. Trend Iconography (MA Momentum)

Alongside each MA level is a directional arrow. This iconography does not represent the current price direction, but rather the momentum of the moving average itself:

↑ Strongly Rising: High bullish momentum baked into recent closing prices.
↗ Rising: Mild bullish drift; structural trend remains intact.
─ Flat: Market consolidation; neutral directional bias.
↘ Falling: Mild bearish drift; structure weakening.
↓ Strongly Falling: High bearish momentum; aggressive sell pressure.

3. The Matrix Heatmap (Multi-Timeframe Confluence)

The Matrix is a high-density, 180-cell confluence map. It visualizes all 10 MA periods across all 18 timeframes simultaneously. This global view allows for rapid identification of macroeconomic alignment.

  • Bright Green Cells: Extremely bullish structure on that specific timeframe and period.
  • Dim Green / Red Cells: Price is tightly hugging the MA line (testing support/resistance).
  • Bright Red Cells: Extremely bearish structure.

Go-to-Market Trading Strategy: Look for vertical or horizontal color uniformity. An entire column turning red indicates a specific timeframe has flipped completely bearish, warning of a potential trend reversal.

4. Live Crossover Scanner

The bottom panel continuously monitors 810 possible MA relationships (45 pairs × 18 timeframes) in real-time, instantly surfacing critical structural shifts.

  • ▲ Golden Cross (Bullish): A shorter-period MA crosses above a longer-period MA. Indicates accelerating short-term momentum.
  • ▼ Death Cross (Bearish): A shorter-period MA crosses below a longer-period MA. Indicates decelerating short-term momentum.

5. Core Technology & Data Pipeline

The dashboard is powered by a proprietary Node.js WebSocket proxy architecture connected directly to the Delta Exchange API. All data relies on Simple Moving Averages (SMA)—calculated as the arithmetic mean of the last N closing prices, ensuring true unweighted mean representations.

Timeframes marked with a grey dot (e.g., 10m, 45m, 6h) are synthetically constructed via client-side resampling of native 1m candles, providing deep granularity without additional API latency overhead.

Rows above the amber line sit above the live price (resistance); rows below sit under it (support). Bar length shows distance from price. Values are Simple Moving Averages (MA) — arithmetic mean of the last N closes. MA values update on each new candle close for the selected timeframe.
